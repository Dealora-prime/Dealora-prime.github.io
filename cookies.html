Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how Dealora Prime uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve your experience.

Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: needed for basic features like remembering your theme (light/dark) preference.
  • Affiliate cookies: placed by partner stores (e.g., Flipkart, Amazon) when you click a "View Price" link, so they can credit referrals.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use our site.

How to Control Cookies

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that some parts of the website may not work properly without cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

When you follow our links to partner stores, those stores may set their own cookies. Please review their cookie policies for details.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
